# Lokerin Store Listing Draft

## Short Description
Track your job applications, interviews, follow-ups, and notes in a calm offline workspace.

## Full Description
Lokerin helps you manage your job search without spreadsheets or messy notes. Save job applications, track pipeline status, log interviews, add salary ranges, set follow-up dates, and export or restore your data as JSON.

Built for solo job seekers, Lokerin keeps your records local on your device and works offline by default.

## Key Points
- Offline-first job application tracker
- English and Indonesian language options
- Light and dark appearance modes
- Application status filters
- Interview logs with date and time
- JSON export and restore
- Local SQLite storage
