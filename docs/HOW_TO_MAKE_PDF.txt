How to make a PDF from the user guide (Windows, beginner-friendly)
====================================================================

You already have two versions in the "docs" folder:

  1) USER_GUIDE.md   — for developers / editing
  2) USER_GUIDE.html — best for making a PDF on your PC

Steps (HTML → PDF)
------------------

1. Open File Explorer and go to:

   jobapply-tracker\docs\

2. Double-click **USER_GUIDE.html**  
   (It should open in Microsoft Edge or Chrome.)

3. Press **Ctrl+P** on your keyboard (this means "Print").

4. Under "Printer" or "Destination", choose one of these:
   - **Microsoft Print to PDF**   (common on Windows), OR
   - **Save as PDF**              (if you use Chrome)

5. Click **Print** or **Save**.

6. Pick a file name (for example: **JobApply-Tracker-User-Guide.pdf**) and save it wherever you like.

That PDF is yours to send to testers, put on your website, or attach to your Play Store listing notes.
