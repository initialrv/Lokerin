# JobApply Tracker — User Guide

**Version 1.0** · This app helps you keep a private list of jobs you applied for. Your information is saved **on your phone** (or tablet), not on a website you log into.

---

## What this app is for

- Remember **which company** you applied to and **what role** it was.
- Track **where you are** in the process (for example: Applied, Interview, Offer).
- Add **notes**, **links**, **follow-up dates**, and **interview** entries.
- **Export** a backup file when you want (you choose where it goes: email, cloud drive, etc.).

---

## First time you open the app

1. Wait a moment while the app **prepares its storage** on the device. This is normal.
2. If something goes wrong, you may see a **Try again** button — tap it once or twice.
3. You will see **three tabs** at the bottom: **Overview**, **Applications**, and **Settings**.

---

## The three main tabs

### Overview

- Shows **counts** for each stage (Draft, Applied, Screening, and so on).
- Shows a short list of **recently updated** applications.
- Use this tab when you want a **quick snapshot** of your job search.

### Applications

This is where you spend most of your time.

- **Search box** — Type part of a company name, job title, notes, or location. The list updates to match.
- **Colored chips** (Draft, Applied, All, etc.) — Tap a chip to **filter** the list by that stage. Tap **All** to see everything.
- **List** — Each row is one application. Tap a row to open **details**.
- **Plus (+) button** — Tap to **add a new application**.

### Settings

- **Connectivity** — This is **information only**. The app works **without internet** for normal use.
- **Export JSON** — Creates a **backup file** you can save somewhere safe. See “Exporting a backup” below.
- **Privacy** — Reminder that data is stored on the device.

---

## Adding a new application

1. Go to the **Applications** tab.
2. Tap the **+** button.
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Company** — Required.
   - **Role / title** — Required (for example: “Customer Support Specialist”).
   - **Status** — Tap the row to pick a stage (for example **Applied**).
   - **Optional fields** — You can leave these blank:
     - **Applied date** — You can type a date like `2026-05-15` (year-month-day).
     - **Location** — City, remote, hybrid, etc.
     - **Job posting URL** — Link to the job ad.
     - **Compensation notes** — What they pay or what you expect (free text).
     - **Next follow-up** — When you want to check in again (optional date/time text).
     - **Notes** — Anything you want to remember.
4. Tap **Save application**.
5. You return to the list and should see your new entry.

**Tip:** If Save does nothing, scroll up and check that **Company** and **Role** are filled in.

---

## Opening and editing an application

1. On **Applications**, tap the row you want.
2. You see the **detail** screen with the same fields as when you added it.
3. Change anything you need.
4. Tap **Save changes**.
5. A small message may confirm that it saved.

### Deleting an application

1. Open the application’s **detail** screen.
2. Tap **Delete** (top area of the screen).
3. Confirm when asked. **This cannot be undone** on the device.

---

## Interviews

On the **detail** screen, scroll to **Interviews**.

- Tap **Add** to log a new interview (or call, or meeting — you choose the title).
- You can add:
  - **Title** — Required (for example: “Phone screen with recruiter”).
  - **When** — Optional (you can type a date/time in plain text).
  - **Notes** — Optional.
- Tap **Save interview**.
- To remove one, tap the **✕** on that interview row and confirm.

---

## Exporting a backup (JSON)

**Why export?** If you get a new phone, reset the device, or uninstall the app, you may lose data unless you backed it up.

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Tap **Export JSON**.
3. Your phone will show the normal **Share** screen (the same kind you see when sharing a photo).
4. Pick a destination you trust, for example:
   - Your **email** (send it to yourself),
   - **Google Drive**,
   - **Downloads** folder,
   - Or another app you use for files.

**Important:** The backup file may contain **private** information. Only share it with places you trust.

**Note:** On some **web browser** versions of the app, export may not work the same way as on a phone. If you rely on backups, use your **Android** device for exports.

---

## Working without internet

- You can **view**, **add**, **edit**, and **delete** applications **without Wi‑Fi or mobile data**.
- **Export** uses your phone’s share screen; you only need a connection if the app you pick **requires** internet (for example, uploading to a cloud service).

## Simple troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|--------|-------------|
| List is empty | Tap **All**, clear the **search** box, or tap **+** to add your first application. |
| I saved but don’t see it | Go back to **Applications** and pull to refresh if your version supports it; otherwise leave the tab and open it again. |
| Export doesn’t appear | Make sure you are on **Android** (recommended). Try again after closing other apps. |
| App says database error | Tap **Try again** on the error screen. If it keeps failing, restart the phone and open the app again. |

---

## Privacy in plain language

- Your job applications are stored in a **database file on the device**.
- The app does **not** send your list to a company server **by default** (unless you or a developer adds that later).
- **You** control exports when you tap **Export JSON** and choose where to send the file.

---

## Getting help from whoever built your copy

If someone customized the app for you, ask them:

- Where to report **bugs**,
- Whether they added **online accounts** or **ads** (that would change privacy),
- How to **restore** from a JSON backup if they added an import feature.
