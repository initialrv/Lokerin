# Privacy Policy for Lokerin

Effective date: May 19, 2026  
Last updated: May 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lokerin handles information when you use the Lokerin mobile application. Lokerin is developed by Ravenduck.

Lokerin is designed as a local-first job search tracker. The app does not require an account, does not use a developer-operated server, and does not intentionally send your job search data to Ravenduck.

## Privacy Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Lokerin's privacy practices, contact:

**Ravenduck**  
Email: ravenduck.contact@gmail.com

## Information You Enter Into the App

Lokerin lets you save job search information locally on your device. Depending on what you choose to enter, this may include:

- Company names
- Role or job titles
- Job locations
- Application statuses
- Applied dates and follow-up dates
- Job posting links
- Salary range notes
- Personal notes
- Interview logs, interview dates, and interview notes
- App preferences, such as language, theme, notification settings, and onboarding state

This information is created by you and stored on your device.

## Information Lokerin Collects

Lokerin does not collect your personal data on a developer-operated server.

Lokerin does not require:

- An account
- Your name
- Your email address
- Your phone number
- Payment information
- Location tracking
- Advertising identifiers
- Analytics tracking

Lokerin does not sell user data.

## Local Storage

Lokerin stores app data locally on your device using local app storage, including local database storage and app preference storage.

Because your data is local, Ravenduck cannot access, recover, edit, or delete your in-app data for you. If you lose your device or delete the app without exporting a backup, your Lokerin data may be lost.

## Export and Import

Lokerin includes export and import features so you can back up or move your data.

You may export your data as files such as JSON, CSV, or PDF. Exported files are created on your device and may be shared using your device's share options. If you choose to save or share an exported file through another app or service, such as email, cloud storage, messaging, or file managers, that third party will handle the file according to its own privacy policy.

You may import a JSON backup file into Lokerin. Lokerin reads the file you select for the purpose of restoring your app data.

Ravenduck does not receive your exported or imported files unless you choose to send them manually.

## Calendar Access

Lokerin may ask for calendar permission if you choose to add an interview or follow-up reminder to your device calendar.

Calendar access is used only to create calendar events that you request. Lokerin may access available device calendars as needed to create the event. Calendar events are stored in your device calendar app or calendar account, depending on your device settings.

Lokerin does not send your calendar data to Ravenduck.

If you add an event to a third-party calendar service, such as a Google account calendar, that calendar provider may process the event according to its own privacy policy.

## Notifications and Reminders

Lokerin may ask for notification permission so it can show local reminders, such as follow-up reminders or backup reminders.

Lokerin uses local notifications for reminders on your device. Lokerin does not intentionally collect push notification tokens and does not use remote push notifications from a Ravenduck server.

You can turn notifications on or off in Lokerin settings and in your device settings.

## Files and Device Sharing

Lokerin may use your device's file and sharing features when you export data, import a backup file, or share a PDF or CSV file.

Lokerin only reads files that you select for import. Lokerin only creates export files that you request. Lokerin does not scan your device files.

## Internet Access

Lokerin is designed to work offline and does not require a Ravenduck server for normal app use.

Some app builds or platform libraries may include internet permission as part of the mobile framework, development client, or operating system features. Lokerin does not use this permission to upload your job search data to Ravenduck.

If you use your device's share sheet to send exported files through another online service, that service may use internet access independently of Lokerin.

## Data Sharing

Lokerin does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

Lokerin does not share your in-app job search data with Ravenduck or third parties, except when you choose to use device features that involve another app or service, such as:

- Saving an exported file to cloud storage
- Sending an exported file by email or messaging app
- Adding an event to a calendar account
- Opening or sharing files with another app

In those cases, the third-party app or service you choose is responsible for how it handles the data.

## Data Retention

Your Lokerin data stays on your device until you delete it, overwrite it through an import, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Archived applications remain stored locally unless you delete or overwrite the data.

Exported files remain wherever you save or share them. Deleting data inside Lokerin does not automatically delete exported files stored outside the app.

Calendar events created from Lokerin remain in your calendar unless you delete them from the calendar app or account.

## Data Deletion

You can delete or remove data in the following ways:

- Delete individual application records in the app, if available
- Archive or restore application records
- Import a backup that replaces existing data, when using the app's import flow
- Delete exported files from wherever you saved them
- Delete calendar events from your calendar app or account
- Clear Lokerin app data from your device settings
- Uninstall Lokerin from your device

Because Lokerin does not operate user accounts or cloud storage, Ravenduck cannot delete local device data remotely.

## Security

Lokerin is designed to reduce data exposure by keeping your job search data on your device.

Your data is protected by your device's operating system and app sandbox. Lokerin does not add separate cloud encryption or server-side security because it does not operate cloud storage for your app data.

You are responsible for protecting your device and exported backup files. We recommend using a device lock screen and being careful when sharing or storing exported files.

## Third-Party Services and Libraries

Lokerin is built with Expo and React Native libraries that provide mobile app functionality such as local storage, notifications, calendar access, file export, and sharing.

Lokerin does not use third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs for user profiling.

If you use external apps or services through your device, such as calendar providers, cloud storage, email, or messaging apps, their privacy policies apply to the data you choose to share with them.

## Children's Privacy

Lokerin is not directed to children under 13 years old and is not intended for use by children. Lokerin does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. When it changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.

If changes are significant, they may also be noted in the app, store listing, or release notes where appropriate.

## Summary

Lokerin is a local-first app. Your job search data is stored on your device. Ravenduck does not operate a server to collect your Lokerin data, and your data is only shared outside the app when you choose to export it, share it, import it, or add it to another device service such as your calendar.
